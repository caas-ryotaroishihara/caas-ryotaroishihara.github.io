about

Ryotaro Ishihara (b. 2001, Japan) is a composer and sound artist based in Yokohama, Japan. Working across acousmatic music, electroacoustic composition, sound installation, and algorithmic practice, his work explores the thresholds between perception and imperceptibility, site and memory, documentary and fiction. He graduated from the Music Studies program at Tokai University in 2024.

His works have been presented in Japan and internationally. In France, his pieces have been performed at acousmonium concerts organized by Nathanaëlle Raboisson in Romainville, and at the Conservatoire national supérieur de musique et de danse in Villeurbanne. In Japan, he has performed regularly at Born Creative Festival (Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre) since 2022, presenting acousmatic and electroacoustic works including Tailing and Isôura. In 2024, he released the album Tailing. As a film composer, he has contributed music and sound to works by director Kodai Takahashi (Mansui), including the short film A Veil (2023) and the mid-length film Tower of Driftwood (2026).
